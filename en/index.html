Three books. Two authors. One direction.
Cosmologica
The Open Universe
Getting to the bottom of the cosmic network — with three surprises: The cosmos is still young. A single spectrum runs through everything — from the cosmic web to brains to language. And the universe is older than its Big Bang: something was there before the limits of the visible, and it left traces.
3rd edition available · Deutsch · English
Droidica
Robotics, artificial intelligence and the next stage of evolution
Rising complexity is no accident — it is the impulse. We follow this principle to its outermost consequence: technospheres are not a threat, they are the next chapter of a story that began with the first biological organism. The next dominant species will not be born — it will be built.
In preparation · 2026
Euclidica
Space telescopes, dark energy and the large-scale structure of the universe
ESA's Euclid space telescope is the century project scientists have been waiting for. It will measure countless galaxy positions more precisely than humans have ever been able to see. The largest 3D map of the universe will answer several open questions in physics — finally! We accompany this astronomical marvel of engineering on its journey and make sense of the groundbreaking results.
In preparation · 2027

Further Out
Two books. Bolder questions.
1/f — Signal Intelligence
Pink noise: the pattern in heartbeat, music and starlight
Between order and chaos lies the realm we live in. No rhythm is entirely uniform, no signal entirely random — a pattern runs through our world like a red thread: in the heartbeat, in music, in sleep, in space. A hundred-year-old riddle that no one has fully explained to this day.
In preparation · 2026
Alien Technologies
Signal transmission, technosignatures and the discovery of extraterrestrial life
How do we recognise a message? What if the first extraterrestrial encounter looks different from what we expect — and who will we be dealing with? Evolution suggests that universal laws create similar life forms, and that we would recognise part of the alien. Yet in the end it may not be us speaking with the strangers, but their AI with ours.
